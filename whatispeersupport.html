What is peer support Info session

Want to learn more about the Peer Support Program? Join a 1-hour virtual info session on June 17, 2026. The Peer Support team will present the services available to support your well-being.

Who can attend?

The session is open to anyone interested in learning more about the program, including:

  • members of the Defence community affected by sexual misconduct
  • families and supporters
  • service providers and mental health professionals/li>
  • leaders and colleagues
  • for group services, be willing to take part in a virtual discussion with a Peer Support Program staff before the first group session

You are welcome to ask questions or simply listen. Sessions are held on MS Team in English and French. To join, click on the time that works best for you.
