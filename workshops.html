Who can join

To participate, you must:

  • be a current or former Canadian Armed Forces (CAF) member, Veteran, Department of National Defence (DND) public service employee, Cadet, Junior Canadian Ranger, or part of the Defence Community
  • have experienced sexual misconduct during your service or in the defence environment
  • have access to a computer, tablet, or smartphone with a stable internet connection
  • have access to Microsoft Teams, either through the desktop/mobile app or a supported web browser (note: Safari is not supported)
  • for group services, be willing to take part in a virtual discussion with a Peer Support Program staff before the first group session

Earth Rhythm Meditation and Writing

This seasonal meditation and writing workshop uses meditation and writing techniques to help you slow down, pause and reconnect with yourself and your surroundings.

Each session starts with a guided meditation to quiet your mind and body and help you focus on the present moment. Writing is used as a mindfulness activity practice to help you notice and explore what feels important to you.

This workshop follows the changing seasons. As nature moves through times of growth, rest and renewal, the workshop helps you notice how your own changes too.

You do not need writing experience to participate. You can take part at your own pace. This workshop is about being present and aware rather than writing perfectly or producing finished work.

  • Duration: 90 minutes
  • Language: English and French
  • Hosted by: A certified yoga instructor and a peer supporter may be present

Peer activity: Zentangle – Creative Coping

Join a 90‑minute virtual session to explore a simple drawing activity inspired by Zentangle®. During this mindfulness activity, you will use easy, repeating patterns to help you slow down and focus on the present moment. No art experience is needed. This session is open to all peers and offers a relaxed and supportive space. You can take part in a way that feels right for you. All you need is paper and a pen. Follow along at your own pace.
